## 📌 Basic Statistical Measures

### 1️⃣ Mean (Average Score)

Mean is the average of all marks.

**Formula:**

Mean = (Sum of all marks) / (Number of students)

**Calculation:**

= (78 + 85 + 78 + 92 + 67 + 85 + 74 + 90 + 78 + 60) / 10  
= 787 / 10  
= **78.7**

👉 **Average Score = 78.7**

---

### 2️⃣ Median (Middle Value)

Step 1: Arrange marks in ascending order

60, 67, 74, 78, 78, 78, 85, 85, 90, 92

Step 2: Since total students = 10 (even number)

Median = Average of 5th and 6th values  
= (78 + 78) / 2  
= **78**

👉 **Median Value = 78**

---

### 3️⃣ Mode (Majority Category – Gender)

Mode is the most frequently occurring value.

Gender count:
- F (Female) → 5 students  
- M (Male) → 5 students  

👉 **Mode = No single mode (Both genders are equal)**
